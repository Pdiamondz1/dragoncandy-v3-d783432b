Design Decisions

  • Reviews conditional: Entire section hidden when total_reviews === 0 — no empty reviews component
  • Review cards: Uses existing PublicProfileReviews component (already has star ratings, reviewer info, dates) — just wrapped in conditional render
  • Dual CTA: "Hire This Creator" (primary, teal) opens the existing ContactCreatorModal · "Message" (secondary, outlined) also opens it — same action, different intent framing
  • CTA copy: "Hire This Creator" is more action-oriented than "GET IN TOUCH" — clearer intent for brands
  • Sticky consideration: CTAs stay at the bottom of the scroll — no sticky footer needed on mobile since the page is short
