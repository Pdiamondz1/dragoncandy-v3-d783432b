Design Decisions

  • Heading: "JOIN DRAGON CANDY" + subtitle "How will you use Dragon Candy?" — clear intent
  • Business card: Teal border (#4DD9C0), restaurant/store icon, teal-tinted icon background
  • Creator card: Pink border (#F9A8D4), camera icon, pink-tinted icon background
  • Card layout: Horizontal — icon (56px, rounded-2xl) + title/description + chevron arrow
  • Descriptions: Action-oriented — tells users what they'll DO on the platform
  • Tapping a card → navigates to signup form with role pre-set (business_client or content_creator)
  • "Already have an account? Log in" at bottom — mirrors the login page toggle
  • Back navigation: Browser back button or "Log in" link returns to login view
