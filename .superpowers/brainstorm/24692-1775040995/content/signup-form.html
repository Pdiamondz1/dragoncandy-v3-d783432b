Design Decisions

  • Same teal-to-pink gradient background — consistent across login, role selection, and signup form
  • Heading: "CREATE ACCOUNT" — distinct from login's "WELCOME TO DRAGONCANDY"
  • Role badge: Pill showing selected role (icon + label) with a "Change" link to go back to role selection
  • White card container matches login page styling
  • Fields: Full Name, Email, Password — same 48px/rounded-lg/gray-100 styling as login
  • CTA: "Create Account" button (teal, full-width pill) — distinct from login's "Login" label
  • Social auth + divider — same pattern as login page
  • reCAPTCHA renders below the form (not shown in mockup but stays in its current position)
  • No inline role picker — the radio buttons from the current AuthForm are removed since role is pre-selected
