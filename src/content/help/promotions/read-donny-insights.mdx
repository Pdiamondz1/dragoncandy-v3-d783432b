# Reading Donny's Toast Insights

Once Toast is connected, Donny can pull real data from your POS — menu performance, traffic patterns, and redemption history — to give you campaign suggestions grounded in what's actually happening at your restaurant. This brief explains what Donny sees and how to get the most out of it.

## What Donny knows

Donny's Toast insights cover three areas over the most recent 30-day window:

### Menu performance
- Which menu items are synced
- How many successful sync events occurred
- The most recent item count from your Toast menu

This helps Donny recommend promotions around specific dishes — "your loaded nachos had the most orders last week, let's build a promo around them."

### Traffic patterns
- Order volume broken down by day of week and hour
- Peak vs. slow periods

Donny uses this to suggest timing: "Tuesday 2–4pm is your slowest window — a flash promo could fill those seats."

### Redemption history
- How many promotion codes were redeemed through Toast
- Success vs. failure counts per promotion
- First and last redemption timestamps

This tells Donny which past campaigns actually drove POS transactions — not just submissions, but real spending.

## How to ask Donny

You don't need to remember specific commands. Just talk naturally:

- "What's selling best this week?"
- "When's my slowest day?"
- "Suggest a campaign for my new dessert menu"
- "How did last month's 15% off promo perform?"

Donny will call the Toast insights tool behind the scenes and respond with specific data points — item names, days, hours, counts.

## The pink CTA button

On the Promotions page, you'll see a pink **Ask Donny for campaign ideas** button. Tapping it opens Donny with the prompt "Suggest three campaigns based on my Toast data" already loaded. It's the fastest way to get started.

## When Donny says "I don't have Toast data yet"

This means one of two things:
1. **Toast isn't connected** — head to Settings → Integrations and connect first (see the [Connect Toast brief](/help/promotions/connect-toast))
2. **No sync events yet** — Toast was just connected and data hasn't populated. Give it about 15 minutes after connecting, then try again.

Donny will never guess or make up menu items. If the data isn't there, it tells you honestly.

## Tips for better results

- **Be specific** — "What promo would work for my burger menu on weeknights?" gives Donny more to work with than "give me ideas"
- **Mention timeframes** — "this week" vs "last month" helps Donny focus on the right data window
- **Follow up** — after Donny suggests a campaign, say "create it" and Donny will set it up for you directly
- **Check back weekly** — as more data accumulates, Donny's suggestions get sharper

## Data freshness

Toast data syncs continuously while your connection is active. The insights view always reflects the last 30 days. If you notice stale data, check the Toast card in Settings — if the status shows "Error" or "Expired", the connection needs refreshing.
