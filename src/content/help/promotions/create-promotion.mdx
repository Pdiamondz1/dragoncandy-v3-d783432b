# Create Your First Promotion

A promotion is your way of turning customers into content creators — they shoot a quick video or photo at your restaurant, and you reward them with a discount. This brief covers everything from first click to live QR code.

## Before you start

Make sure you have:
- A business account on DragonCandy (creator accounts can't publish promotions)
- At least one active location in your profile
- An idea for what you're promoting — a new menu item, a slow weeknight, a seasonal special

## Step 1 — Open the Promotions tab

From your business dashboard, tap **Promotional Tools** in the sidebar (or the QR icon in the bottom nav on mobile). You'll land on the Promotions tab showing any existing promotions.

Tap the **+ Create Promotion** button at the top.

## Step 2 — Fill in the basics

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| **Title** | Short, punchy — this is what customers see on the submission page (e.g. "Show us your brunch!") |
| **Description** | One or two sentences explaining what you want them to capture |
| **Discount type** | Percentage or fixed amount |
| **Discount value** | The reward — e.g. 15% or $5 |
| **Max duration** | Video length cap in seconds (default 30s) |
| **Start / end dates** | When the promotion is live |

You can also set **Max redemptions** to cap how many discount codes get issued.

## Step 3 — Choose accepted content types

Pick whether you want video only, photo only, or both. Photos are great for quick "snap and go" promos; video captures more of the vibe.

## Step 4 — Publish

Hit **Publish** and DragonCandy generates a unique QR code and shareable link. Print the QR, stick it on your table tent or receipt holder, and customers scan it to enter.

## What customers see

When a customer scans your QR code they land on a mobile-friendly submission page with your logo, discount badge, and a three-step flow:

1. Record/upload their content
2. Enter name, email, phone, and optional social handles
3. Submit — they get a "pending review" confirmation

## Reviewing submissions

Submissions land in your **Pending** tab. You can preview the video/photo, see the customer's social handles, then approve or reject. Approved submissions automatically trigger a discount code sent via email and SMS.

## Tips for a great promotion

- **Keep it specific** — "Film yourself trying our new spicy wings" beats "Post about us"
- **Match the reward to the ask** — a 30-second video deserves more than 5% off
- **Set a tight window** — urgency drives participation
- **Ask Donny** — tap the pink "Ask Donny for campaign ideas" button and Donny will suggest promos based on your actual Toast sales data
