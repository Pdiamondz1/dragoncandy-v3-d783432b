# The Customer Submission Flow

This brief explains what your customers experience from the moment they scan your QR code to the moment they receive their discount. Understanding this flow helps you set expectations with staff and troubleshoot any questions on the spot.

## How customers find your promotion

There are two entry points:
1. **QR code** — printed on table tents, receipts, window stickers, or menus
2. **Direct link** — shareable via social media, email, or text (e.g. `dragoncandy.io/promo/abc123`)

Both land on the same mobile-optimized submission page. No app download required — it's a standard web page that works in any phone browser.

## The three-step submission

### Step 1 — Welcome screen

The customer sees your restaurant logo, the discount badge (e.g. "15% OFF"), and a brief description of what you're asking for. Three bullet points explain:
- Record a video or take a photo
- Enter their details
- Get a discount code

They tap **Let's Start** to continue.

### Step 2 — Capture content

Tapping the upload area opens their phone's camera or file picker. They can:
- **Record live** — the browser camera opens, limited to your configured max duration
- **Upload from library** — choose an existing video or photo

Accepted formats: MP4, MOV, WebM for video; JPG, PNG, HEIC for photos. Max file size is 50 MB.

Once they select a file, they advance automatically.

### Step 3 — Enter details

The form collects:
- **Name** (required)
- **Email** (required — where the code gets sent)
- **Phone** (required — SMS backup delivery)
- **Social handles** (optional) — Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, YouTube

Social handles let you tag them when you repost their content. These are stored in your review panel so you can find them later.

They check the marketing rights box and tap **Submit**.

## After submission

The customer sees a confirmation screen explaining:
- Their submission is pending review
- Once approved, they'll get a discount code via email and SMS
- They show the code at checkout to redeem

## Your side — what happens in the background

1. The video/photo uploads to secure storage
2. A submission record appears in your **Pending** tab
3. You preview, then approve or reject
4. On approval: DragonCandy generates a unique discount code and sends it to the customer's email and phone
5. If Toast is connected (Partner tier): the code also syncs to your POS automatically

## Duplicate protection

DragonCandy prevents the same email or phone number from submitting twice to the same promotion. If a customer tries again, they see "You have already submitted for this promotion." This protects you from code farming.

## FAQ

**Can customers resubmit if rejected?**
Yes — if you reject a submission, they can scan the QR again and try with new content.

**What if the promotion is full?**
Once max redemptions is hit, new visitors see "This promotion has reached its maximum number of redemptions."

**Do customers need a DragonCandy account?**
No. The submission flow is fully anonymous — no sign-up required.
