# The 48-hour test — outreach script

> **Goal:** get ONE Hoboken restaurant to pay a flat fee for 3 creator videos this week with a trackable
> promo code, and measure redemptions. This is the cheapest possible test of the reshape's core bet
> (restaurants pay for a *tracked-covers* outcome). Success = they hand you money; the deep signal =
> whether the promo code gets redeemed.

## Who to hit first

Owner-operated, single-location, does *some* social already (so they value it) but clearly struggles at
it (inconsistent posts, low effort). Busy enough to have slow nights they'd love to fill. Walk in at
**2–4pm** (between lunch and dinner rush) and ask for the owner/manager by name if you can get it.

## The channel that works: in person > DM > email

Restaurant owners live in their restaurants and ignore inboxes. **Walk in.** Use DM/email only to follow
up someone you've already met or been referred to.

---

## In-person pitch (60 seconds, then shut up)

> "Hey [name] — I'm [you], I'm local, I run DragonDash here in Hoboken. Quick one: I get local creators
> to make short videos of your food and I post them for you — but the part nobody else does is I **track
> it to actual diners**, not likes. Here's what I'll do: **3 videos this week, one flat price, and I put
> a promo code on them so you can literally count how many people came in from it.** No contract, no
> monthly anything — just this week, and you see the numbers. If it works, we talk about doing it every
> month. If it doesn't, you're out one flat fee and you keep the videos. Want to try it?"

Then **stop talking.** The next person to speak loses. Let them react.

## The close (the number)

- One-time flat fee for **3 videos this week + a tracked promo code + a one-page report**. Test price:
  **$250–$350 one-time** (tune it — you want a *yes with real money*, not a free pilot).
- If they hesitate on price: *"Fair — how about $[lower], and if the report shows it worked, your first
  month is $399 flat?"* (Anchor them toward the monthly plan on a good report.)
- Get **cash / card / Stripe link on the spot** if you can. A "yes I'll think about it" is a no.

## Follow-up DM / email template (only after you've met them)

> Subject: 3 videos this week — and we count the diners
>
> Hi [name] — great meeting you at [place]. Recapping the offer so it's easy to say yes to:
>
> **This week:** 3 short creator videos of your food, filmed by a vetted local creator you approve,
> posted across your channels. Every post carries a **promo code**, so you get a one-page report of how
> many diners it actually drove — not views.
>
> **One flat fee, no contract, no monthly.** If the report shows it worked, we can set up a simple $399/mo
> plan. If not, you keep the videos and we part friends.
>
> Can I lock in a creator for [day]? Reply "go" and I'll send the Stripe link.
>
> — [you], DragonDash · [phone]

## The three objections you WILL hear (and the honest answers)

1. **"It's too much / I don't have the money."**
   → *"That's exactly why it's one flat fee and one week, not a subscription. You risk [$X] once and you
   see real numbers before committing to anything."* (Do not discount to free — a free pilot proves
   nothing about willingness to pay.)

2. **"I don't trust some random person filming my food."**
   → *"You approve the creator before they show up — I'll text you their last 5 videos and their local
   following, and you pick. Nobody films without your yes."* (This is finding #5 — reliability + control.)

3. **"Does this actually bring people in? I've had viral posts do nothing."**
   → *"Honestly, that's the whole reason I put a promo code on it. I'm not going to show you views and
   call it a win — you'll see exactly how many redemptions came in. If it's zero, you'll know, and so
   will I."* (This is the wedge — you're the only one selling *proof*, not vanity.)

## What to actually track (this is the point)

- **Did they pay?** (yes/no + price) — the primary signal.
- **Promo-code redemptions** over the 2 weeks after posting — the deep signal (did content → covers).
- **Would they do a monthly plan after seeing the report?** — the retention leading-indicator.

Log every attempt (pitched / paid / redeemed / renewed-intent). Ten honest walk-ins tells you more than
any amount of platform building. If you can't get *one* owner to pay for a tracked-covers outcome in 48
hours, that is the most valuable — and cheapest — thing you could learn this month.
