# DragonDash Local — the one-page offer

> The buyer's #1 objection was *"give me ONE number, not four line items."* This is that offer — one
> price, one outcome (covers), no agency games. Prices below are **test-and-tune** starting points, not
> final. Everything internal (creator payout, take-rate, AI cost) is your COGS and never appears here.

---

## Proven diners from social. Done for you.

**You run the restaurant. We run your social — and we prove it put people in seats.**

Local creators film your food where it happens, we post it across your channels, and every month you
get a **Covers Report**: how many diners a promo code from your content actually brought in. Not views.
Diners.

### What you get — one flat price

**$399/month. That's it.**

- **4 creator videos a month**, filmed at your restaurant by vetted local creators
- **Posted for you** across Instagram, TikTok & YouTube (we handle captions, scheduling, hashtags)
- **A monthly Covers Report** — a unique promo code on every post, tracked to real redemptions
- **You approve every creator** before they set foot in your door — see their last 5 videos + local
  following first
- **No setup fee. No per-video charge. No surprise "AI" fees. Cancel anytime.**

### How it works (about 15 minutes of your time a month)

1. **15-minute kickoff** — tell us your vibe, your hero dishes, your slow nights.
2. **We shortlist local creators** — you tap approve on the ones you like.
3. **They film, we post** — you do nothing.
4. **You get your Covers Report** — see what actually drove redemptions, and we tune next month around
   what worked.

### Why this isn't another agency

- **We measure diners, not vanity.** A promo code ties every post to a real redemption — you'll never
  wonder "did that do anything?"
- **You're in control.** You approve every creator and every concept. Your reputation, your call.
- **One honest price.** No line-item stack, no "reach" packages, no lock-in.

### Our promise

We guarantee **delivery and honest measurement** — creators show up, footage is usable, and you get a
real Covers Report every month. If a creator no-shows or the footage isn't usable, we re-shoot it free.
We won't promise a magic diner number — we promise to **show you what the content drove and keep
changing the play until the numbers move.** (We can't fix your food or a rainy Tuesday; we can prove
what social did.)

## Notes for us (not for the customer)

- **Price test plan:** start at $399/mo; A/B $299 vs $399 vs $499 across the first cohort. Track which
  price the *renewals* cluster at, not which price gets the most first-yeses.
- **The Covers Report is the product.** Even a crude v1 (unique promo code → Toast redemption count) beats
  a views dashboard. This is the attribution spine from the reshape plan — build it first.
- **The re-shoot guarantee** covers *delivery* quality (reliability = half the product), NOT a covers
  number — do not over-promise covers; the literature says restaurant-content ROI is genuinely hard, which
  is exactly why *measuring* it is the moat.
- **48-hour test = the wedge in.** Lower commitment, same proof loop; converts to the monthly plan on a
  good report. See `./outreach-script.md`.
