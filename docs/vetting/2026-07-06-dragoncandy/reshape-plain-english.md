# DragonCandy, explained simply (with Joe's notes baked in)

## What DragonCandy does (in one breath)

Restaurants want more customers. DragonCandy gets local video-makers to film short, fun videos of a
restaurant's **real food and real place**, posts them online, and helps bring more diners in.

## The big thing we learned

Restaurants **will** pay for this — that part is real. But if we sell it the wrong way, they'll try it
once and quit. So the whole game is selling it the *right* way. Think of a lemonade stand: making good
lemonade isn't enough — you have to make people come back and buy again.

## The rules (simple version)

**1. One price. That's it.**
Don't hand a restaurant a confusing pile of charges (a monthly fee + a creator fee + a rush fee + a
robot fee). That feels like a sneaky phone bill. Just say: *"It's $X a month. Done."* One number they
can trust.

**2. Count customers, not likes.**
A video with a million "likes" but zero new diners is useless to an owner. So put a **coupon code** in
every video. When people use it, the restaurant *sees* "10 people came in from this video." Real
customers — not internet points. **This is the most important part, because nobody else does it.**

**3. Do it FOR them.**
Restaurant owners are slammed. So DragonCandy does the whole thing — find the creator, film it, post
it, send the report. The owner spends 15 minutes, not 15 hours.

**4. Let restaurants pick their own creators. (Joe's note — 100% right.)**
Restaurants should be able to **look through ALL the creators** — like flipping through trading cards —
and pick who they want. If an owner would rather have the app suggest someone, that's an **option** too
("surprise me"). But we **never** hide the creators or force the app to choose for them. The restaurant
is always the boss. DragonCandy helps — it doesn't gate-keep.

**5. Real videos of THEIR real food and place. (Joe's note — and it's a secret weapon.)**
The heart of DragonCandy is a real person filming the restaurant's **real** food, kitchen, and vibe.
That's the good stuff. Robot-made ("AI") videos can be an **extra option** if a restaurant wants one —
but they're not the main thing. Here's the cool part: **a robot can't film YOUR actual restaurant.** So
"real footage of your real place, with proof it brought people in" is exactly what the cheap copycats
and the AI apps can't fake. That's a big advantage — it's the thing we can win on.

**6. Start in ONE town. Make it work. Then grow.**
Don't try to be everywhere at once. Pick Hoboken. Get a handful of restaurants. Make sure they're happy
enough to **pay again next month.** Once that works, use the same recipe in the next town. (Sign up the
video-makers first, then the restaurants.)

## The first tiny test (do this week)

Before building anything fancy, do the simplest thing possible: get **ONE restaurant to pay for 3
videos this week**, put a coupon code on them, and count how many people come in. If a restaurant pays
real money for that — the plan works. If not, we learned it super cheaply.

## Why DragonCandy can win

Real videos of real restaurants **plus proof that real customers showed up.** Copycats can make videos.
Robots can make videos. But *"authentic footage of your actual place, and here's proof it filled
seats"* is really hard to copy. **That's the thing to own.**
