# 🐉 Ten DragonCandy Slogans

> Captured from an internal Donny answer on 2026-08-11, in response to:
> "If you came up with slogans, ten of them, about how Dragon Candy is an ad agency disruptor and a workplace within a content marketplace that delivers content fast; what would you come up with?"

Got everything I need — brand voice, origin story, existing taglines, the DragonDash verb strategy, the agency disruption angle, the "less typing = more margin" north star, and the speed-as-product philosophy. Here are ten slogans built directly from the DNA of DragonCandy.

---

## 🐉 Ten DragonCandy Slogans

---

**1. The agency is dead. Long live the Dragon.**
*— Pure disruption. Announces the category shift without hedging. Works as a launch headline.*

---

**2. Your content. Your creators. Delivered before lunch.**
*— Anchors the workplace + marketplace + speed angle in one breath. Concrete and credible.*

---

**3. Fire your agency. Hire Donny.**
*— The most confrontational version. Direct comparison, clear replacement. Works great for paid social targeting restaurant owners.*

---

**4. A campaign in 60 seconds. Content by tonight.**
*— Speed-forward. Speaks directly to the DragonDash value prop. Answers the "how fast?" question immediately.*

---

**5. The marketplace where creators work and restaurants win.**
*— Covers both sides of the platform. Positions it as a living workplace, not just a tool. Inclusive, warm.*

---

**6. Agencies charge $5,000 a month. We charge $149. And we're faster.**
*— The most honest, aggressive pricing disruptor line. Not for every surface, but devastating in the right context — comparison ads, pitch decks, cold email subject lines.*

---

**7. Don't brief an agency. Just ask Donny.**
*— Echoes the existing "Just ask Donny" brand line while explicitly calling out the agency replacement. Bridges the brand voice with the disruption message.*

---

**8. Creators ready. Content fast. No agency required.**
*— Three punches: supply, speed, disruption. Clean enough for a hero subhead or billboard.*

---

**9. The content studio that never closes, never invoices late, and never misses a deadline.**
*— Hits the three biggest agency pain points in one line. Speaks the language of a frustrated restaurant owner or brand manager.*

---

**10. Stop outsourcing to agencies. Start DragonDashing.**
*— Plants the verb. This is the verbification play — uses "DragonDashing" as the action that replaces "hiring an agency." Every time someone says it, it's free brand advertising.*

---

## The Ranking if You Had to Pick One

For **general brand use**: **#1** — "The agency is dead. Long live the Dragon." It's bold, memorable, and category-defining.

For **paid social / performance marketing**: **#3** — "Fire your agency. Hire Donny." Stops the scroll, makes the ask explicit.

For **product hero / homepage**: **#4** — "A campaign in 60 seconds. Content by tonight." Specific, credible, speed-forward.

For **verb seeding / long-term brand building**: **#10** — "Stop outsourcing to agencies. Start DragonDashing." Every impression plants the verb that will eventually replace a category.
