## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 PART 1 — THE HUMAN MARKETING TEAM

Fully staffed with real people, no AI agents filling seats.

---

### The Full Marketing Department — 8 People

**1. VP of Marketing / Head of Growth**
- Reports to Joe (CRO)
- Owns the entire marketing function, budget, and KPIs
- Oversees all three metros, brand narrative, and performance
- Hired: Month 1 — this is the most critical marketing hire
- Salary: **$130–160K base** / ~$170–210K loaded
- Profile: Someone who has scaled a marketplace or creator economy brand before. Not a traditional ad agency person — needs to understand community-led growth

**2. Social Media Manager**
- Owns DragonCandy's Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and X presence daily
- Creates and posts all organic content — Reels, carousels, Stories, TikToks
- Manages the DragonCandy creator spotlight series
- Films creator/restaurant content in the field (Hoboken, Manhattan)
- Hired: Month 1
- Salary: **$55–70K** / ~$72–90K loaded
- Profile: A creator themselves — someone who shoots, edits, and posts natively on TikTok and Instagram. Not a scheduler, a creator

**3. Creator Community Manager**
- The person creators talk to. Owns onboarding, ambassador program, creator Discord/broadcast channel, monthly office hours, and creator retention
- Manages the Dragon Ambassador program day-to-day (see Part 2)
- Is the voice in every creator DM
- Hired: Month 1
- Salary: **$50–65K** / ~$65–85K loaded
- Profile: Has been a creator themselves or worked inside a creator community (YouTube, TikTok). Empathetic, fast responder, community-native

**4. Restaurant & Brand Partnership Manager (Hoboken + Manhattan)**
- Hits the streets. In-person visits to restaurants, attends food industry events, builds local partnerships with restaurant associations, food festivals, and hospitality groups
- Owns the restaurant side of the ambassador program
- Primary hunter for restaurant logos — target 5 new restaurants per week
- Hired: Month 2
- Salary: **$65–80K** + performance bonus / ~$85–105K loaded
- Profile: Knows the Hoboken/NYC restaurant scene. Has hospitality or food industry relationships. Charming, in-person presence

**5. Content Producer / Videographer**
- Shoots and edits all video content for DragonCandy's own channels — not for clients, for marketing
- Case study videos, creator spotlights, launch videos, event coverage, product demos
- Also documents DragonCandy campaigns in action (behind-the-scenes content that becomes marketing gold)
- Hired: Month 2
- Salary: **$60–75K** / ~$78–98K loaded
- Profile: Food and lifestyle videographer. Comfortable on TikTok format. Can shoot and edit same-day

**6. Paid Media Manager**
- Owns all paid social — TikTok Ads, Meta Ads, Google
- Manages targeting, creative testing, budget allocation across metros
- Tracks CAC by channel, reports weekly on what's working
- Hired: Month 3 (after organic is proven, before Manhattan scale-up)
- Salary: **$70–90K** / ~$91–117K loaded
- Profile: Performance marketer with TikTok and Meta experience. F&B or marketplace vertical preferred. Data-driven, not creative-first

**7. SEO & Content Strategist**
- Builds out city-specific SEO landing pages (Hoboken, Manhattan, Palm Beach + future cities)
- Writes blog content, email newsletters, and case study write-ups
- Owns the restaurant owner education content ("why your restaurant needs social media")
- Hired: Month 3–4
- Salary: **$60–75K** / ~$78–98K loaded
- Profile: SEO-native writer with marketplace or local business content experience

**8. Palm Beach Market Manager** *(Metro 3 Launch)*
- Dedicated local hire for Palm Beach — knows the market, the restaurants, the seasonal hospitality scene
- Runs the creator and restaurant recruitment playbook in South Florida
- Owns local events, partnerships, and in-market presence
- Hired: Month 9–10 (ahead of the Palm Beach winter season push)
- Salary: **$55–70K** / ~$72–90K loaded
- Profile: South Florida hospitality industry connection. Bilingual (English/Spanish) is a strong plus for the Palm Beach market

## 🐉 PART 2 — THE DRAGON AMBASSADOR PROGRAM

This is the most powerful growth channel DragonCandy has — and it costs a fraction of paid acquisition. Here's the full offer structure.

---

### The Three Ambassador Tiers

**🥉 Dragon Scout — Entry Level**
- *Requirement:* Complete 3 campaigns on DragonCandy with 4.0+ rating
- *Who:* Any active creator who wants to recruit
- *What they do:* Refer other creators, tag restaurants, post about DragonCandy

**Scout Incentives:**
- **$75 cash** for every creator they refer who completes their first paid gig
- **$150 cash** for every restaurant they refer that completes their first campaign
- **$200 cash** for every brand they refer that signs up and pays
- Priority matching on new campaigns in their area (queue jumped)
- "Scout" badge on their DragonCandy profile — signals credibility to restaurants
- Free 1-month Starter tier upgrade

---

**🥈 Dragon Knight — Mid Level**
- *Requirement:* 10+ completed campaigns, 4.5+ rating, referred 3+ creators or 1+ restaurant
- *Who:* Power creators and micro-influencers (5K–50K followers)
- *What they do:* Active ambassador — posts regularly, attends events, mentors Scouts

**Knight Incentives:**
- **$100 cash** per creator referral who completes first gig
- **$250 cash** per restaurant referral that completes first campaign
- **$400 cash** per brand referral that signs up
- **+15% earnings bonus on all gigs for 6 months** — permanent for top performers
- **Featured creator spotlight** on DragonCandy's social channels (massive exposure)
- Exclusive "Knight" branded merch kit (hoodie, lens kit, portable light — tools of their trade)
- Direct line to the Creator Community Manager — VIP support
- Early access to new features and campaign types
- Free Growth tier subscription (~$449/mo value) for 3 months

---

**🥇 Dragon Master — Elite Level**
- *Requirement:* 50+ campaigns, 4.8+ rating, referred 10+ creators or 5+ restaurants, active ambassador for 6+ months
- *Who:* Top creators, local food influencers, hospitality industry connectors
- *What they do:* City captains — they ARE the brand in their market

**Master Incentives:**
- **$150 cash** per creator referral
- **$350 cash** per restaurant referral
- **$600 cash** per brand referral
- **Recurring revenue share: 3% of platform fees** generated by every restaurant they referred, for 12 months — this is the big one. If they refer a restaurant doing $500/mo in campaigns, they earn $15/mo passively
- **Revenue share on creator earnings:** 1% of every gig completed by creators they referred, for 6 months
- **$1,000 annual "Dragon Master" grant** for content creation equipment or education
- Co-branded content with DragonCandy — their face is on our marketing
- "City Captain" title for their metro — exclusive, one per neighborhood
- Invitation to the annual DragonCandy Creators Summit (travel + hotel covered)
- Free Pro tier subscription (~$899/mo value) for the duration of their Master status

---

### How It Works for Restaurants

Restaurant owners who refer other restaurants get a separate, simpler offer:

**Restaurant Referral Program:**
- **$200 account credit** for every restaurant they refer that signs up and completes a campaign
- **1 free DragonDash delivery** for every 3 referrals
- "Founding Partner" badge on their profile — displayed publicly, signals trust to creators
- Feature in DragonCandy's "Restaurants We Love" social series

---

### How It Works for Brands

Brands who refer other brands (common in the regional food/bev industry):

**Brand Ambassador Offer:**
- **$500 account credit** per brand they refer that signs up
- Co-branded case study featuring both brands — published to DragonCandy's channels
- Invitation to the DragonCandy Brand Council — quarterly input on platform features

---

### The Ambassador Program Budget (Year 1)

Based on aggressive targets — 200 creators referred, 50 restaurants referred, 10 brands referred:

- Creator referral payouts (avg $100 × 200): **$20,000**
- Restaurant referral payouts (avg $250 × 50): **$12,500**
- Brand referral payouts (avg $400 × 10): **$4,000**
- Knight merch kits (20 Knights × $150/kit): **$3,000**
- Master equipment grants (5 Masters × $1,000): **$5,000**
- Creators Summit (travel + hotel, 10 Masters): **$15,000**
- Tier upgrade comps (free subscriptions): **$8,000**
- **Total Ambassador Program Year 1: ~$67,500**

Compared to paid acquisition CAC of $500–$1,500 per restaurant — at $250 per referred restaurant, **the ambassador program is 2–6× cheaper than any paid channel.**

## 💰 PART 3 — AGGRESSIVE YEAR 1 MARKETING BUDGET

Three metros: **Hoboken → Manhattan → Palm Beach**. This is the full-send version.

---

### Metro 1: Hoboken, NJ (Months 1–6)

- Founder-led restaurant outreach (Joe + Partnership Manager): **$0 incremental**
- Launch event — creator mixer at a Hoboken restaurant (food, venue, filming): **$5,000**
- Paid social — Instagram/TikTok targeting NJ food creators + restaurant owners: **$8,000**
- Creator seeding (pay 10 showcase campaigns at $300 avg): **$3,000**
- Local PR — Hoboken/NJ food press, NJ.com, Hudson Reporter pitch: **$3,000**
- Printed collateral (one-pagers, QR codes for restaurants): **$1,500**
- Hoboken food festival sponsorship: **$2,500**
- **Hoboken total: $23,000**

---

### Metro 2: Manhattan, NYC (Months 5–12)

This is the flagship market. NYC has the highest CPM but the biggest creator pool and restaurant density in the country.

- Paid social — TikTok + Meta (primary spend, NYC-targeted, F&B audience): **$60,000**
  - TikTok: $35K (CPM ~$8–12, food category; targets micro-creators + restaurant owners)
  - Meta/Instagram: $25K (CPM ~$12–18 NYC; restaurant owner targeting)
- NYC launch event — rooftop creator mixer, 100 attendees: **$12,000**
- Influencer seeding — pay 5 NYC food influencers (50K–200K followers) to post about DragonCandy: **$15,000**
- Restaurant association partnerships (NYC Hospitality Alliance, etc.): **$5,000**
- Creator showcase campaigns (20 campaigns at $300 avg): **$6,000**
- PR agency retainer (3 months, NYC food/tech press): **$18,000**
- OOH micro-placement — subway/bus ads in food-heavy neighborhoods (Williamsburg, LES, Hell's Kitchen): **$10,000**
- NYC food festival and market sponsorships (Smorgasburg, etc.): **$8,000**
- **Manhattan total: $134,000**

---

### Metro 3: Palm Beach, FL (Months 10–12, winter season push)

Palm Beach is seasonal — the money is in the October–April winter season. Time the spend accordingly.

- Paid social — Meta/Instagram (Palm Beach affluent demographic, hospitality-heavy): **$20,000**
- Palm Beach food/hospitality events (Food & Wine, restaurant weeks): **$8,000**
- Local influencer seeding (Palm Beach food influencers, lifestyle creators): **$8,000**
- Restaurant outreach (Partnership Manager + Palm Beach Market Manager in-person): **$5,000**
- Launch event — Palm Beach restaurant grand opening tie-in: **$5,000**
- Local PR (Palm Beach Daily News, Miami Herald food section): **$4,000**
- **Palm Beach total: $50,000**

---
