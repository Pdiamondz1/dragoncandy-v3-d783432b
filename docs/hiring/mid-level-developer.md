# Mid-Level Full-Stack Developer — DragonCandy

**You would work the audit backlog, reviewed by our senior developer, and grow into owning part of
the system.**

**Location:** Europe is fine. Working hours 12:00–18:00 Central European (06:00–12:00 US Eastern).
**Engagement:** Paid two-week trial → 6-month contract → extend or convert to permanent.
**Start:** Target Q4 2026, 30 days after the product manager and designer.
**Reports to:** the Senior Developer.

---

## The company

DragonCandy connects businesses with content creators. A restaurant gets a social media team
without hiring one. A creator gets real paid partnerships instead of chasing gig apps. Our built-in
AI, **Donny**, writes the campaigns, matches people, and schedules and publishes posts.

Live at dragoncandy.com, with an iPhone app already running on a real device. Pre-revenue by
choice, about 30 organic users, no launch date set.

---

## What you'd do

- Work through the audit backlog — real, defined tickets, not vague ones.
- Fix bugs across the whole product.
- Have every change reviewed by the senior developer, plus two automated AI reviews.
- Take ownership of one area of the system over time.

This is a job where you will be reviewed carefully and taught deliberately. If you want to be left
alone to ship unsupervised, take the senior role or a different company.

## What we need

### Required

- **Solid React and TypeScript.** You are comfortable in a real codebase and you write types
  properly.
- **SQL you can defend.** You do not need deep PostgreSQL expertise on day one, but you must
  genuinely want to learn it — our security lives in the database, and you cannot avoid it here.
- **Care.** This product moves people's money. We would rather have someone careful and slower than
  someone fast and loose.
- **Fluency with AI coding assistants: Claude Code, Claude Cowork and OpenAI Codex.** Every branch
  is reviewed by Codex before a human may approve it. This is the pipeline, not a preference.
- Roughly **3+ years** building software.

### Bonus

- Cursor
- Supabase, Deno, Stripe, or Capacitor
- Experience on a product real customers paid for

## The first 90 days

**Weeks 1–2 — paid trial.** One real ticket, end to end, paid at full rate.

**Month 1.** Work the audit backlog. Learn the codebase by fixing it. Ship several small changes
through the full pipeline.

**Months 2–3.** Take a subsystem. Start being the person who knows one area better than anyone
except the senior developer.

---

## How we work

- **Two-week cycles.** Monday planning, Friday demo.
- **Linear** for tickets, synced with GitHub.
- **Every change:** branch → pull request → automated checks (build, types, linting, 2,443 tests) →
  preview site → AI review → senior review → live.
- **Written and asynchronous by default**, with about three hours of live overlap daily.
- **Done means done:** merged, checked on phone and desktop, verified in production.
